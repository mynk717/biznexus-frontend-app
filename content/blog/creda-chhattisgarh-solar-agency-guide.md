  <h2 id="what-is-creda">1. What is CREDA Chhattisgarh?</h2>
  <p>Established under the Department of Energy, Government of Chhattisgarh, CREDA is the designated agency that implements state and central renewable energy schemes. Whether you want to reduce your <a href="/blog/cg-bijli-bill-pay-online-solar-yojana" class="text-blue-600 hover:underline">CG Bijli Bill</a> with a home solar plant, or you are a farmer needing a solar water pump, CREDA is the governing authority that facilitates the subsidies and regulates the vendors.</p>

  <h2 id="rooftop-subsidy">2. Rooftop Solar Subsidies via CREDA</h2>
  <p>Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residents of Raipur, Bhilai, Bilaspur, and other CG cities are eligible for massive subsidies. However, there is a catch that many homeowners miss:</p>
  
  <div class="p-6 bg-blue-50 dark:bg-blue-900/20 border-l-4 border-blue-500 rounded-r-xl my-6 not-prose">
    <h4 class="text-sm text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 font-bold mb-2">The "Empanelled Vendor" Rule</h4>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm">To receive the additional State Top-Up Subsidy (up to ₹30,000) on top of the Central subsidy, your solar installation <strong>must be completed by a CREDA-empanelled vendor</strong>. Installing through an unregistered agency will disqualify you from the state benefits.</p>

  <h2 id="saur-sujla">3. Saur Sujla Yojna (Solar Pumps for Farmers)</h2>
  <p>One of CREDA's most successful initiatives is the Saur Sujla Yojna. This scheme provides highly subsidized solar irrigation pumps (3HP and 5HP) to farmers in Chhattisgarh, particularly in areas where the electrical grid has not yet reached.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Who is eligible?</strong> Farmers across Chhattisgarh. Preference is given to areas without active grid connectivity.</li>
    <li><strong>How to apply:</strong> Applications are processed through local agriculture department offices or directly via the official CREDA portal.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 id="vendor-registration">4. Vendor & System Integrator Registration</h2>
  <p>If you run a solar business and want to participate in government tenders or offer subsidized rooftop solar to residential clients, you must register with CREDA as a System Integrator.</p>
  <p>The vendor empanelment process usually opens periodically. Businesses must submit technical credentials, financial turnover records, and pay an empanelment fee to be listed on the official CREDA vendor directory.</p>
